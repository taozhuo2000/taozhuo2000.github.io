I am **Zhuo Tao (陶卓)**, a Ph.D. student in Computer Science and Technology at the [Institute of Computing Technology, Chinese Academy of Sciences](http://www.ict.ac.cn/) (ICT, CAS) and the University of Chinese Academy of Sciences. I am affiliated with the Key Laboratory of Intelligent Information Processing.

My research focuses on building intelligent systems that can perceive, reason, learn, and act. I am particularly interested in **large language models (LLMs)**, **intelligent agents**, **reinforcement learning**, **multimodal learning**, and **vision-language models (VLMs)**.

我是**陶卓**，中国科学院计算技术研究所计算机科学与技术专业直博生，所在实验室为中科院计算所智能信息处理重点实验室。我的研究兴趣包括**大语言模型、智能体、强化学习、多模态学习与视觉语言模型**，关注能够进行感知、推理、学习与行动的通用智能系统。

Research Interests / 研究方向
======

- Large Language Models and Vision-Language Models / 大语言模型与视觉语言模型
- Intelligent Agents and Agentic Reinforcement Learning / 智能体与智能体强化学习
- Multimodal Understanding and Reasoning / 多模态理解与推理
- Reinforcement Learning and Post-training / 强化学习与模型后训练
- Video Understanding and Temporal Localization / 视频理解与时序定位
