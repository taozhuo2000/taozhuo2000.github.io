We propose **QACap**, a question-aware captioner that uses the question to extract relevant visual evidence before an LLM performs knowledge-based reasoning. The method improves the alignment between visual observation and external knowledge in knowledge-based visual question answering.

我们提出问题感知图像描述框架 **QACap**，利用问题引导模型提取相关视觉证据，再交由大语言模型完成知识推理，从而改善知识型视觉问答中视觉观察与知识推理之间的对齐。
