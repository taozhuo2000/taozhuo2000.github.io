We introduce **COTEL**, a collaborative temporal consistency learning framework for point-supervised natural language video localization. It jointly improves frame-level saliency modeling, segment-level boundary prediction, and video-text alignment under sparse supervision.

我们提出点监督自然语言视频定位框架 **COTEL**，通过协同时间一致性学习，在稀疏监督条件下联合优化帧级显著性建模、片段级边界预测与视频文本对齐。
