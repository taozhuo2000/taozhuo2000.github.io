We propose **DQR-DETR**, which dynamically recalibrates moment queries for out-of-distribution video temporal localization. Progressive semantic regularization, hierarchical query decoupling, and query recalibration improve coarse-to-fine temporal boundary prediction and generalization.

我们提出面向分布外视频时序定位的 **DQR-DETR**，通过渐进语义正则化、层次化查询解耦和时刻查询重校准，实现动态查询生成与由粗到细的时序边界优化。
